## Read-across

- Only **SFT** reliably moves accuracy in every phase it's tried (01: 0→94%; 02: 38.9%→61.1%).
- **CPT never clears its own bar** (03: null/rejected; 04: no significant delta once SFT is applied) — consistent structural finding, not one bad run.
- **GRPO adds nothing over SFT** (02) — same pattern as CPT: a second training pass on top of SFT keeps failing to beat SFT alone.
- **Spectrum layer selection is the one lever that reliably beats its baseline** (04, fresh arm, every stage significant) — but only on the non-CPT base.
